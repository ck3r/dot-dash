  3. The Morse code TREE

Every character sits at the end of its own path: from the start, a dot goes one way and a dash the other. E and T are a step down, the commonest letters near the top, so the deeper a character, the longer it takes to send. Choose one (or type it) and the tree walks it down, a mark at a time.

    cards
    
    
Each character as a tile, like the periodic table's:

    
┌───────────┐
│num   -----│   what it is, its marks
│     0     │
│22      0 .│   weight, dots
│        5 −│           dashes
└───────────┘

    
What it is: char, num, sym or pro, with cut for a letter that
    stands in for a digit. Its weight: the units it takes to send,
    its gaps and a letter gap included (PARIS is 50, with a word
    gap).

    
With stroke on, the letters and numbers are drawn by their
    marks.
