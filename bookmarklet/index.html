dot-da.sh

a bookmarklet

Select some text on any page, tap a bookmark, and it's on the station's line as Morse. A bookmark that runs a few lines instead of opening a page: nothing to install, and nothing runs until you tap it.

keep it

→ dot-da.sh

On a computer, drag that to your bookmarks bar. (Show the bar with Ctrl+Shift+B, or ⌘+Shift+B on a Mac.)

On a phone, or anywhere dragging doesn't work: , bookmark any page, then edit the bookmark and paste over its address. To use it on a phone, type the bookmark's name in the address bar and pick it from the list.

use it

  • Select some text, then tap the bookmark: the station opens in a new tab with it on the line.
  • Nothing selected, and it asks what to send.
  • Select Morse (-- --- .-. ... .) and it arrives as text.
  • The text rides after the # in the address, which a browser never sends to a server: it goes from the page to the station in your browser, and nowhere else.

lite

It sends, and that's all. Playing on the page, a card by the selection, copying as Morse and keyboard shortcuts need the browser extension, which can stay ready in the background. A bookmarklet can only run when it's tapped.

A few sites don't let bookmarklets run at all; that's their security settings, nothing to fix here. On those, copy the text and paste it at the station.
